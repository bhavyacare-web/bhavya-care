Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

At Bhavya Diagnostics (Main Jhajjar Road, Opp. Narendra Farm, Bahadurgarh, Haryana – 124507), your privacy and health data security are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal and diagnostic information across our website (bhavyadiagnostics.com) and Android mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Contact Information: Full name, 10-digit mobile number (verified via OTP), email address, city, pincode, and residential address for home sample collection.
  • Diagnostic & Health Information: Selected pathology blood tests, health packages, ECG/PFT bookings, uploaded doctor prescriptions, and digital laboratory reports.
  • Payment & Wallet Records: Booking payment status, wallet cashback/referral transactions, and UPI reference screenshots submitted for VIP Membership or KYC payouts.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule and fulfill doorstep home sample collection and center visits.
  • To process diagnostic samples through verified NABL/NABH accredited partner laboratories and deliver your digital PDF reports via your dashboard and email (report@bhavyadiagnostics.com).
  • To manage your Bhavya VIP Family Membership, referral bonuses, and wallet balance.
  • To send booking confirmations, report alerts, and customer support responses.

3. Data Sharing & Confidentiality

We never sell or rent your personal or medical data to third parties. Your relevant booking details (name, age/gender if applicable, address, and test list) are shared strictly with the authorized phlebotomist and the designated NABL/NABH processing laboratory (such as CRL Diagnostics or VED Diagnostics) solely to complete your diagnostic test.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All user authentication, database records, and diagnostic PDF reports are hosted on encrypted Google Firebase cloud infrastructure with strict role-based access controls so only you and authorized diagnostic staff can access your reports.
