Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Bhavya Diagnostics strives to provide reliable and timely diagnostic services in Bahadurgarh. Please review our cancellation and refund terms below:

1. Pathology Blood Tests & Health Packages

  • Cancellation Before Sample Collection: You may cancel your booking anytime before our phlebotomist collects your sample at home or before sample draw at our collection center (Main Jhajjar Road, Bahadurgarh).
  • Instant Wallet Adjustment: If you used Bhavya Diagnostics Wallet balance while booking, the deducted wallet amount is refunded instantly to your wallet upon cancellation.
  • After Sample Collection: Once a blood or urine sample has been collected and registered for laboratory testing, cancellations or refunds are not permitted.

2. Center-Based Tests (ECG & PFT)

  • ECG and Pulmonary Function Test (PFT) appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled prior to the test procedure at the center.

3. Prepaid Refunds & Wallet Withdrawals

1. Bhavya Diagnostics Wallet: Approved order refunds are credited directly to your Bhavya Diagnostics wallet and can be redeemed on future bookings.

2. UPI / Bank Payouts: For eligible prepaid refunds or approved partner wallet withdrawals, transfers are processed to your verified UPI ID within 3–5 working days.

4. Bhavya VIP Family Membership

VIP Family Plan membership fees are non-refundable once activated or once any test booking has been placed using VIP discounted rates.
